Digital Transformation in Hospitality

Marriott's AI Deployment in Singapore

Pioneering smart hotel technology to elevate the guest experience across our Classic and Distinctive brands.

Chatbots & Digital Concierge

Leveraging natural language processing, our AI-powered chatbots facilitate seamless communication. Guests can effortlessly request services, book facilities, and receive instant support directly from their mobile devices, ensuring a highly responsive 24/7 service.

Automated Check-in/Out

Embracing a mobile-first mentality, we deploy automated kiosks and facial recognition technology. This frictionless process allows tech-savvy travelers to bypass the front desk and autonomously access their rooms with digital IDs and mobile keys.

Service Robots

To augment our dedicated staff, autonomous service robots are integrated into our front-end operations. From independently delivering fresh towels to interacting with guests, these intelligent interfaces blend innovation with Marriott's time-honored hospitality.
